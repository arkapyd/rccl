      Road to RCCL

      Seven clauses
      1. Every match is two a side over twenty overs. No schedule and no set number of games — a cycle simply runs for its window.
      2. PCW is wins as a percentage of matches played. It is the only ranking number, borrowed from the WTC — minus the points, since a match here cannot end level.
      3. A tied match goes to a two-over Super Over. Whoever takes it takes the W, exactly as if they had won in regulation.
      4. Each cycle has a win gate (seven by default). Below it you appear on the table but are not in contention.
      5. At the close, the top four by PCW past the gate play a 2v2 final — 1st and 4th against 2nd and 3rd. The final decides the title only and never moves the table. Short of four past the gate, the cycle goes uncrowned.
      6. A pair that forfeits loses and the other pair wins. Everything played up to the forfeit — runs, wickets, overs, 50s — still counts; the match just carries no margin.
      7. If the game falls over mid-innings, the result stands on the D/L par score. The chase must pass par outright — short of it, however narrowly, is a loss.
