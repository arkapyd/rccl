    Road to RCCL

    Six clauses
    1. Every match is two a side over twenty overs. No schedule and no set number of games — the cycle simply runs for its window.
    2. PCW is wins as a percentage of matches played. It is the only ranking number, borrowed from the WTC — minus the points, since a match here cannot end level.
    3. A tied match goes to a two-over Super Over. Whoever takes it takes the W, exactly as if they had won in regulation.
    4. The gate stands at seven wins. Below it you appear on the table but are not in contention.
    5. At the close, the highest PCW among those through the gate takes the cycle. Level on PCW, they settle it 2v2 until it breaks.
    6. If the game falls over mid-chase, the result stands on the D/L par score at the moment it died. The chase must pass par outright — short of it, however narrowly, is a loss.
