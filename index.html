    Laws of the Cycle

    Six clauses
    1. Every match is two a side over twenty overs. No schedule and no set number of games — the cycle simply runs for its window.
    2. A result gives both winners a W and both losers an L. Nothing else about the match is recorded, and that is the point.
    3. PCW is wins as a percentage of matches played. It is the only ranking number, borrowed from the WTC — minus the points, since a twenty-over match cannot be tied.
    4. The gate stands at seven wins. Below it you appear on the table but are not in contention.
    5. At the close, the highest PCW among those through the gate takes the cycle. Level on PCW, they settle it 2v2 until it breaks.
    6. Matches dated outside the window stay in the record but do not count toward the cycle.
