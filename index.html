    How the cycle is won

    • Every match is 2v2 over 20 overs. No fixture list, no fixed count — play whoever's around.
    • A result gives both winners a W and both losers an L. Nothing else is recorded.
    • PCW is wins as a percentage of matches played. It's the only ranking number.
    • The gate: 7 wins minimum to be crowned. Below that you're on the board but not in contention.
    • At the cycle's close, the highest PCW among those through the gate takes it. Level on PCW, they play a decider.
    • Matches dated outside the cycle window don't count toward it.
