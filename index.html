    Laws of the Cycle

    Seven clauses
    1. Every match is two a side over twenty overs. No schedule and no set number of games — the cycle simply runs for its window.
    2. A result gives both winners a W and both losers an L. Runs, wickets and overs may be recorded for the record, but no scorecard figure ever ranks anybody.
    3. PCW is wins as a percentage of matches played. It is the only ranking number, borrowed from the WTC — minus the points, since a match here cannot end level.
    4. A tied match goes to a two-over Super Over. Whoever takes it takes the W, exactly as if they had won in regulation.
    5. The gate stands at seven wins. Below it you appear on the table but are not in contention.
    6. At the close, the highest PCW among those through the gate takes the cycle. Level on PCW, they settle it 2v2 until it breaks.
    7. Matches dated outside the window stay in the record but do not count toward the cycle.
